how it works

From zero to the first message in four steps.

No account, no address book: you and the other person install Cifra and exchange IDs. Everything happens on the two phones.

01

Create your identity

One tap, and the app generates an identity that is yours alone, on the phone. It becomes a 16-character ID and a unique pattern you can recognise at a glance. No personal data is asked for.

the path of a message

Only the two phones can read. The rest of the path sees sealed envelopes.

The cryptography follows Signal’s published protocols, implemented in open code. Nothing home-made.

your phoneSeals the envelopeThe message is encrypted with a fresh key for every send. The sender goes inside the envelope, and every envelope has the same size: from the outside, a long message looks just like a “hi”.
tor networkHides where you connect fromOrbot carries the envelope through the Tor network. The server receives it without learning your location on the internet.
serverHolds it without knowing whoseIt leaves the envelope in the recipient’s box and deletes it once their phone confirms it was saved. It only sees that a box received something, and the hour. If nobody picks it up in 14 days, it is deleted undelivered.
tor networkAgain, no addressThe recipient also fetches their box through the Tor network.
recipient’s phoneOpens the envelopeOnly here do the message and the sender appear.

Photos, videos and files (up to 25 MB) travel separately, encrypted the same way and kept for the same period. Of those, the path only sees the approximate size.

features

You decide what stays and what goes.

Each option explains, in the app itself, what happens when you turn it on. Nothing depends on a cloud.

hidden chats

Gone from the list. Back with a code.

Choose a secret code and hide any chats you want: they leave the list, the search and the notifications. To see them again, type the code into the search field. Without it, nothing on screen suggests they exist.

panic

Hold the button. Everything goes.

In under two seconds, Cifra destroys the keys and erases chats, contacts and settings on this phone; with a connection, it asks the server to delete your ID and your box. There is no undo: the identity only returns with the 12 words, and whatever you saved to the gallery or exported as a backup stays where it was.

security

What Cifra protects — and what it does not.

No app is “100% secure”. This is the map of what it does and what depends on you.

protects
The contentOnly the two phones can read. Server and network see sealed envelopes, all the same size.
Who talks to whomThe server does not keep who sent each message, and the Tor network hides where each person connects from.
The locked phoneChats live in an encrypted database that only opens with your fingerprint or passcode. Someone who takes the locked phone reads nothing — and what you deleted does not come back.
The key exchangeThe safety number shows if someone has stepped between you and your contact.
does not protect
Whoever received the messageThey can keep it, copy it or photograph the screen with another device.
A tampered app on the other sideDisappearing messages, delete for everyone and screenshot blocking depend on the other person running the original Cifra.
An unlocked phone in someone’s handsWith the app open, whoever looks at the screen sees. Auto-lock and hidden chats reduce the damage.
The fact that you use TorYour carrier does not see who you talk to, but it sees that you use the Tor network and when the app is active.
tested before release

During development of this version, the app went through 12 acceptance tests on an emulator — among them a forensic examination with IPED, the Brazilian Federal Police’s forensic analysis tool:

locked phone · nothing readable across 36,000 items examined network · tor traffic only, no address exposed server database · no content, sender or address

The tests have not yet been repeated on a physical device, and the code has not had an independent security audit. Cifra is new: keep that in mind.

Free, no ads, no tracking. For now, the installer (APK) is downloaded directly from here.

Download the APK you will need Android 10 or newer with a PIN, pattern or password screen lock. Without a screen lock the app does not store the identity; if you remove the lock later, the chats on this phone are lost. Orbot, a free, open-source app that connects the phone to the Tor network. Without it, Cifra does not connect. Someone on the other side with Cifra: it cannot talk to people on other apps.

Orbot is available at orbot.app, on Google Play and on F-Droid.

how to install
1Install Orbot and connect it to the Tor network.
2Download Cifra’s APK and open the file. Android warns that it comes from outside the store: that is normal. Allow installation only for the browser you used.
3Open Cifra. With Orbot running, the chat list shows “tor · connected”. If it shows “tor · unavailable”, open Orbot again.
4Write down the 12 words and exchange your ID with whoever you want to talk to. New versions install over the old one, and your chats stay.

faq

Frequently asked questions.

Do my friends need to install it too?Yes. Cifra only talks to Cifra: each person installs the app and Orbot on their Android phone, and you exchange IDs. It cannot message people on WhatsApp or any other app.
Why do I need Orbot? Is it safe?Cifra only connects through the Tor network, and on Android Orbot is what makes that connection — a free, open-source app available at orbot.app, on Google Play and on F-Droid. There is no mode without Tor.
Does it work on iPhone? Are there groups, calls or voice messages?Not yet. Today Cifra is Android only and one-to-one only, with text, photos, videos and files. There is no date for the rest.
I changed or lost my phone. Now what?With the 12 words, your identity comes back on the new device and the old one stops receiving immediately. With an exported backup, contacts and settings come back too. Messages stay on the old phone: they do not migrate. Without the words, the identity is lost: create a new one and tell your contacts.
What does the server keep, and what can its operator see?Only what has not been delivered yet, encrypted and without a sender; once delivered, it is deleted, and whatever nobody picks up is gone in 14 days. The operator sees that an ID exists, when its box receives something, when the app is connected and the approximate size of files. Not the content, the sender, your location or your contact list — and nothing is logged.
Can I trust it? Has the code been audited?There has been no independent audit yet, and the tests ran on an emulator. On the other hand, everything is open: anyone can read the app and server code and check the file they install. It is a new app; treat it as one.
How much does it cost?Nothing. No ads, no tracking, no paid plan. The project is supported by donations.
Can I run my own server?The server code is open and anyone can run a copy, but the app in this version only talks to the official server.
